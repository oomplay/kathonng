## Technical Notes

- All models are in **glTF 2.0 Binary (.glb)** format.
- Models are pre-scaled and bounded for use with the `fitModelToConstraints` utility in `src/lib/gltf.ts`.
- The `visitor.glb` includes skeletal animations: `idle`, `walk`, `run`, `jump`, `fall`, `wave`, `punch`, `death`, `cheer`, `dance`.
- Model URLs in the asset registry (`ASSET_REGISTRY`) use the `/models/` base path served by Vite from `public/models/`.
